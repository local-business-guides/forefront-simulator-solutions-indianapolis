A short checklist before you call

A little preparation helps make the first conversation more useful. You don’t need every detail settled, but gather what you can:

  • Identify the room or area you’re considering, such as a basement or garage.
  • Share the approximate room dimensions if you know them.
  • Decide who will use the simulator and whether golf is the only activity you have in mind.
  • Note any existing features you’d like to keep, such as a finished area or storage.
  • Collect a few photos of the space so you can discuss its layout.

Avoid choosing equipment before talking through the room. A simulator experience should be considered in context with the space, so ask how the equipment and the planned layout will work together.
