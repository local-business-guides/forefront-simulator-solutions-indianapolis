Questions that help you compare providers

Before you make a decision, use a short checklist to keep the conversation focused:

  • What information do you need about my room before recommending a layout?
  • Which design, equipment integration, and finishing details are included in the proposed project?
  • How will the setup support the way I plan to use the space?
  • If I want golf plus another simulator activity, can those goals be discussed together?
  • Which decisions should I make now, and what can be worked out during planning?

Be cautious about choosing based only on equipment names or a polished concept. A design that sounds appealing still needs to suit the room and your day-to-day plans. Also avoid leaving assumptions unstated. Write down what you expect the finished space to include and ask the provider to review that list with you.
