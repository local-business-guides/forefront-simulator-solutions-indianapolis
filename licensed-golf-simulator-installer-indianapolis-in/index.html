What to prepare before a project conversation

A little preparation makes it easier to discuss what will fit. Gather the details you already know, and bring questions about anything that still needs checking.

  • Identify the room or area you’re considering, such as a basement, garage, or business space.
  • Share how you plan to use the simulator, including whether golf is the main focus or you want other simulator activities too.
  • Note any existing room plans, measurements, or photos you can provide.
  • Ask what equipment integration and finishing work are included in the proposed scope.
  • Confirm who handles any work that may require separate trade qualifications or approvals.

A common mistake is settling on equipment before checking whether the room plan supports the experience you want. Another is assuming that one installer automatically covers every construction or trade task. Get clear answers before work begins.
