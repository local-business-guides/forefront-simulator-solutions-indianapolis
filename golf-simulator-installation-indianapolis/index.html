Start with the room and how you’ll use it

Golf Simulator Installation Indianapolis is a project worth planning around the room, not just the equipment. Think about who will use the simulator, what kinds of play you want, and whether the space also needs to serve as a family room, office, or garage. Those answers help shape a setup that fits your home rather than taking over the room.

Before you hire a contractor, gather a few basics:
- Room length, width, and ceiling height
- Photos of the space from several angles
- Any doors, windows, beams, or built-ins that affect the layout
- A list of the activities and equipment you have in mind

Measurements are a starting point, not a substitute for evaluating the room in person. If you’re unsure whether a swing can fit comfortably, have the space assessed before ordering equipment.

What to prepare, and what to avoid

Before a planning conversation, clear enough floor space to show the room’s dimensions and note anything that cannot be moved. If you’re converting a garage or basement, share what else you need to keep in that area. That helps set practical priorities early.

A common mistake is choosing equipment before confirming the room layout. Another is overlooking details such as where people will stand, how they’ll enter and exit, and whether lighting or existing features interfere with the setup. Don’t assume a room is ready based on floor area alone. Bring up changes to walls, power, or other building elements during planning so the right work can be discussed with the appropriate professionals.
